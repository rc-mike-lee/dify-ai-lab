# Acme Support Knowledge Base

## Resetting a password
Customers reset passwords from the sign-in page via "Forgot password". The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check spam, confirm the account email, then trigger a new link. Agents must never read out or set a password for a customer.

## Billing: invoice totals
An invoice can exceed the plan price because of added seats, usage overages, or proration after a plan change. Compare the invoice line items with the plan in the CRM. Billing corrections over 50 USD need a supervisor.

## Billing: refunds and chargebacks
A refund is issued by us to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. A chargeback is a dispute the customer raises with their bank; we must not issue a refund while a chargeback is open. Double charges are refunded in full after confirming both transactions.

## Payments: declined cards
Common reasons: expired card, insufficient funds, a billing address mismatch, or the bank's fraud check. Ask the customer to confirm the card details or contact their bank, then retry. Never ask for or repeat a full card number.

## Plans and seats
Basic: up to 5 seats. Pro: up to 50 seats, priority support. Enterprise: unlimited seats, SSO (SAML), dedicated manager. Seat changes take effect immediately and are prorated.

## SSO setup (Enterprise)
SAML SSO needs the IdP metadata URL, the entity ID, and a verified email domain. Escalate SSO problems to Tier 2 with the IdP name and the error text.

## Data export
Account owners can export all data from Settings > Data > Export. Exports are emailed as a ZIP link valid for 24 hours.

## Shipping and delivery
Hardware ships within 3 business days. Tracking is emailed at dispatch. Orders not shipped after 5 business days should be escalated to fulfillment.

## Handling personal data
Only share personal data with the verified account owner. Do not paste SSNs, dates of birth, or full addresses into replies. Verify identity with the account email and the last four digits of the card on file.
